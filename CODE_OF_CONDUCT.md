# Code of conduct

Be kind and be patient. Cajoa is for beginners as much as for experts, and questions are welcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Help others learn. Do not mock a question or a mistake.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing private information, and unwanted attention.

## Reporting

Report a problem by using GitHub's private reporting on the **Security** tab. Reports are handled privately.

People who break these rules may have their comments removed and may be blocked from the project.

This code of conduct follows the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
